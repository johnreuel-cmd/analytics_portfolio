Background and Overview

Northstar SaaS Solutions is a Philippine-based B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) company that provides cloud-based workflow, analytics, and business operations platforms to growing companies across Asia-Pacific.

The company has approximately 1,000 employees across Engineering, Customer Support, Product, Sales, Operations, Finance, and Human Resources. As Northstar expands its customer base and product offerings, its workforce requirements have also increased, particularly across technical and customer-facing roles.

However, workforce growth has created a growing economic challenge. Northstar is experiencing employee turnover, prolonged vacancies, increasing replacement costs, and continued hiring demand at the same time. Leadership needs to determine whether additional workforce investment should focus on hiring new employees, retaining existing employees, or a targeted combination of both.

The executive team commissioned a Workforce Economics: Hiring vs. Retention Investment Analysis to quantify the financial impact of workforce movement by analyzing hiring investment, vacancy economic exposure, employee turnover, replacement costs, and retention investment.
